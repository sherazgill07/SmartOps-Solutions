A new name for the same work. This studio operated as Zoho Solutions and has since become SmartOps Solutions — a name that reflects the full scope of the work: not just one platform, but the people, processes and technology around it.

Most operational problems aren't technology problems — they're clarity problems. A sales team re-entering the same data three times. A process that lives in one person's head. A CRM that was set up once and never revisited.

I work directly with founders and operations leads to map how work actually happens, then build the systems — CRM setup, workflow automation, dashboards, integrations — that make that work faster and less error-prone.

Every engagement ends with something your team can run without me: documented, owned, and built around how you actually operate.

Where I help

Focused engagements, not open-ended retainers. Pick where the friction is worst.

CRM setup & cleanup

Configuring Zoho and similar CRMs around your actual sales process — pipelines, fields, automations and reports that people will actually use.

Workflow automation

Removing manual, repetitive steps between the tools you already run — quotes, approvals, follow-ups, notifications — so work moves without someone pushing it.

Process mapping & documentation

Turning tribal knowledge into clear, written processes your team can follow and your new hires can learn from on day one.

Systems integration

Connecting CRM, finance, support and project tools so data moves between them automatically, instead of being copied by hand.

Ops consulting

A structured review of how your business runs today, with a prioritized plan for what to fix first and what to leave alone.
